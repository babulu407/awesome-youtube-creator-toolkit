## 📋 YouTube Studio Limits & Specifications Cheat Sheet (2026)

* **Video Title:** 100 characters max *(Recommended: 50–60 characters)*
* **Video Tags:** 500 characters max *(Including commas and spaces)*
* **Video Description:** 5,000 characters max
* **Thumbnail Resolution:** `1280 × 720 px` (16:9 aspect ratio, under 2 MB)
* **Channel Banner:** `2560 × 1440 px` *(Mobile safe area: 1546 × 423 px)*
